Privacy policy – LCT Automation

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses this app

LCT Automation is an internal tool. Only the business owner's own Google accounts sign in to it. It has no public users.

What Google data it uses and why

  • Gmail (the business's operations mailbox): reads incoming case emails and adds labels to mark them as handled; sends the business's own notices.
  • Google Drive: reads and saves case documents in the business's own case folders.
  • Google Sheets: reads and updates the business's own case record sheet.

The data is used only to keep the business's own case records up to date.

Where data goes

  • Data stays in the business's own Google account and on the business's own computer that runs the tool.
  • Some documents may be sent to Google's Gemini service to read the text in them. Nothing is sent to any other service for this.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept on the business's own computer and can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access page.

Google API Services User Data Policy

LCT Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app.
